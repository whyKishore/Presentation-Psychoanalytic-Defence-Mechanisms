# Psychoanalytic Defence Mechanisms and Their Relevance to Social Media

An interactive lecture and presentation exploring psychoanalytic theory, ego defence mechanisms, and their interpretive applications to contemporary social media behaviours.

## 📖 Overview

Psychoanalytic theory explains human behaviour as a response to unconscious conflict and anxiety. This project presents:
- **Freud's Structural Model:** Id (pleasure principle), Ego (reality principle), and Superego (internalised ideals and morality), along with the three forms of anxiety (Objective, Neurotic, and Moral).
- **Core Defence Mechanisms:** Detailed explorations and everyday examples of Repression, Denial, Displacement, Projection, Rationalization, Intellectualization, Reaction Formation, Sublimation, and Regression.
- **Relational & Psychosocial Perspectives:** Karen Horney's theory of basic anxiety and neurotic trends, and Erik Erikson's lifespan stages (identity vs. role confusion, intimacy vs. isolation).
- **Application to Social Media:** Thought experiments and illustrative scenarios exploring how curated profiles, comment sections, and online conflict map conceptually to psychoanalytic defence dynamics.
- **Critical Evaluation:** Discussion of empirical limitations and boundaries between clinical theory and online interpretation.
